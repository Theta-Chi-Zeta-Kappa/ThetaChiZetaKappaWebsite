Share Zeta Kappa History

Tell us what you have and, when useful, attach the historical files. Submissions are reviewed by the website administrators before anything is added to chapter records.

Accepted: ZIP, PDF, Word, text/RTF/Markdown, CSV/Excel, PowerPoint, JPG/JPEG, PNG, and WEBP. Up to 100 MB total per submission.
